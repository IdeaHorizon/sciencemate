# Scientific schematic grammar

Represent nodes, ports, edges, groups, labels, evidence status and reading order explicitly.

- Workflow: one dominant direction, aligned stages, distinguish inputs/outputs/decisions.
- Mechanism: arrows must declare activation, inhibition, transport, association or hypothesis; do
  not use one arrow style for incompatible meanings.
- Apparatus: geometry, components and connections come from a supplied spec or source, never visual
  invention. Dimensions are shown only when supplied.
- Compartments/anatomy: containment and orientation carry meaning; generative decoration cannot be
  evidence.
- Feedback/cycles: route edges to avoid crossings and label delays or signs when supplied.

Use a restrained semantic palette, black text, consistent corners/strokes and generous gutters.
Measured, modeled, procedural and hypothesized elements need a legend or visibly distinct grammar.
